      Message from Founder

      Hey there! I'm Zeke Neill, the Denverite responsible for Scooter.fyi. This project originally grew out of a desire to track and monitor the contractual SLA performance of our new City of Denver micromobility monopoly, Veo. I set out just to collect data and watch the metrics — but between the portrait-mode-only clunkiness of the Veo app (in sharp contrast to the landscape phone holders on the Cosmo, Apollo, and Rover) and the fact that Apollo e-bikes are just plain more fun than the other options (personal opinion!), it turned out there's a lot a web app can do that's complementary to the Veo app itself. So I built the app I found useful, and I hope you get something out of it too. If you do — or there's a feature you'd like to see — please don't hesitate to reach out.

      A few things worth knowing

      This is a beta, experimental project. Features may appear out of thin air, get removed, or break. Don't get too invested in any data in this app.

      I'm not making any money off this — none, and I don't intend to anytime in the near future. I can't promise that as a permanent policy, but we're nowhere near it even being a discussion, and if this ever were to become a for-profit endeavor, that change would be clearly publicized. The goal is simple: build something people genuinely enjoy using. If you want to support the operational costs, the best things you can do are share this app with others and check out my advocacy sites, WeSeeYouVeo.com and KeepDenverFair.com.

      Scooter.fyi will always be pro-consumer. We won't do things that hurt the people using this site. There will never be any sale of data, sketchy third-party analytics, or anything else that harms users — the only measurement here is private, first-party, cookieless analytics we run ourselves, with no third parties involved, ever, and you can switch it off right below. For those of you who have created accounts: thank you for your trust. I take your privacy and data security extremely seriously. The governing documents are here:

      Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

      Private analytics

      First-party and cookieless: no third-party scripts, no analytics cookies, no persistent identifier. Turning this off stops all usage-event collection for this browser; the choice is stored on your device only.
